📊 Key Insights & Business Recommendations

Insights:
• Transaction trends help identify high-performing and low-performing periods.
• Customer segmentation highlights the most valuable customer groups.
• State-wise analysis reveals regions with higher transaction activity and growth potential.
• Transaction status analysis helps identify failed transactions and operational gaps.
• KPI tracking provides a clear view of revenue, fees, tax, and average transaction value.

Recommendations:
• Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments to increase revenue.
• Investigate failed transactions and improve the payment process to reduce customer drop-offs.
• Strengthen business presence in high-potential regions identified through state-wise analysis.
• Introduce targeted offers and personalized services based on customer behavior.
• Monitor KPIs regularly through Power BI to identify trends early and support faster, data-driven decisions.

💡 The goal: Turn financial data into actionable insights that improve customer experience, operational efficiency, and business growth.
